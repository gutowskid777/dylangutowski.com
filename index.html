The One-Pager

Brahva

Prototype  ·  built at the Claude Builders Club hackathon

Hospitals are required by law to publish every price they charge. They post them as files nobody opens. Brahva reads those files and tells you what a procedure will actually cost you, at each hospital near you, on your insurance, before you walk in. The pilot parsed 43,510 real prices out of 12 hospitals across Philadelphia and Westchester. The numbers in the demo below are the real ones.
